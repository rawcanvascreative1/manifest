Section 03

Authentication & SMS One-Time Password (OTP) Verification

To ensure secure access and protect e-commerce purchasing pathways, Manifest implements a multi-channel authentication framework:

  • Google OAuth Integration: When selecting "Continue with Google," we receive standard public profile parameters (verified email and display name). We never access your Google password, contacts, or personal cloud files.
  • Direct Email & Password: Credentials created directly on the studio platform are stored with strict cryptographic hashing. We provide instant self-service ritual password recovery links sent exclusively to your registered inbox.
  • Mobile Phone Authentication with 4-Digit OTP: When logging in via mobile device, a cryptographically generated 4-digit verification code is transmitted via telecom gateway to your mobile number. This code expires within a time-limited window and is used solely to authenticate session legitimacy and prevent unauthorized access.

Under no circumstances will studio representatives contact you demanding your confidential OTP or password.

Section 04

Payment Architecture & PCI-DSS Safeguards

Your financial security is our highest operational priority. Manifest Crystals of Life operates in full compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS):

  • Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay): Card information is processed through secure, bank-grade tokenization tunnels. Manifest Studios never captures, logs, or stores raw 16-digit Primary Account Numbers (PAN) or Card Verification Values (CVV) on our servers or client browser storage.
  • Unified Payments Interface (UPI) & Net Banking: Direct UPI intent requests (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and Net Banking portals are mediated directly by authorized national payment aggregators. No bank account credentials or MPINs ever touch our infrastructure.
  • Pricing & Currency: All transactions are billed in Indian Rupees (₹ INR), inclusive of applicable GST and handling disclosures as detailed at checkout.
Section 05

Client-Side Storage (`localStorage`) & Functional Cookies

To deliver a seamless, stateful single-page experience without intrusive tracking scripts, our application utilizes browser localStorage for critical functional keys:

manifest_user

Retains current active session state, display name, verified authentication method, and membership tier so you remain logged in across studio visits.

manifest_cart

Preserves your curated bracelet selections and custom 3D creations in your bag until checkout completion.

manifest_orders

Stores your local historical ritual receipts and fulfillment numbers for rapid retrieval inside your Studio Profile overlay.

manifest_reg_*

Caches registered account credentials locally for demo and offline persistence within your private browser environment.

You have the autonomy to clear these local storage items at any time through your browser's Developer Tools or Clear Browsing Data utility. Logging out from your profile immediately clears the active session state.
