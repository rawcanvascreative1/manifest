Section 03

Authentication Channels & Mobile SMS OTP

To safeguard your orders and reserve natural minerals securely, checkout is protected by our multi-channel authentication interface:

  • Google OAuth: Allows one-click verified sign-in using standard OAuth protocol. We only receive your public profile identifier and email address.
  • Email & Password: Direct account registration with cryptographic password hashing and automated password recovery links sent to your registered inbox.
  • Mobile Phone Authentication with 4-Digit OTP: When logging in via phone, a temporary 4-digit code is dispatched via telecom gateway to your mobile device to verify ownership in real time. Codes expire automatically and are never stored in plain text.
✦ Security Protocol Notice
Manifest Studios personnel will never initiate calls, SMS, or emails requesting your confidential password or 4-digit verification code.
Section 04

Payment Processing & PCI-DSS Compliance

Financial transactions on Manifest are conducted in Indian Rupees (₹ INR) via PCI-DSS Level-1 certified payment gateways.

  • Zero Card Data Storage: We never capture, transmit, or retain raw 16-digit credit/debit card numbers or 3-digit CVV security codes on our servers or client browser storage.
  • Tokenized Payments: Card transactions (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex) undergo encrypted tokenization directly through certified banking aggregators.
  • UPI & Net Banking: Instant payments via Unified Payments Interface (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and major banking portals occur within bank-grade encrypted channels.
Section 05

Client-Side Storage (`localStorage`)

To ensure an instantaneous, smooth single-page boutique experience without intrusive third-party cookies, Manifest utilizes browser localStorage exclusively for vital functional state:

  • manifest_user: Stores your active login session and display name.
  • manifest_cart: Preserves your curated pieces and 3D customizer selections.
  • manifest_orders: Retains your digital order receipts for instant profile view.
  • manifest_reg_*: Stores client-side demo account credentials securely within your private device environment.

You can purge this data at any moment by signing out from your profile or clearing browsing history in your browser settings.
